📍Phase 2.1 — Product Specification

We've built the Design Language. The next artifact is even more important.

UX Review Companion Product Specification (PRD v1.0)

This document becomes the single source of truth for the product. Whenever we're unsure about a feature, we'll refer back to it.

It will answer:

1. Product Vision

One sentence describing why UX Review Companion exists.

2. Problem Statement

What problem are we solving, and why is it worth solving?

3. Target Users

Not "everyone."

For example:

UX Designers
Product Designers
UX Leads
Product Managers
Design Managers

Each has different goals.

4. User Journey
   Upload
   ↓
   AI Understands Dashboard
   ↓
   Review Generated
   ↓
   Designer Validates Findings
   ↓
   Designer Takes Action
5. Core Product Principles

These come from our Design Language:

Evidence before recommendation
AI explains itself
One card = one question
Scannable in under 2 minutes
Refine, don't redesign 6. Information Architecture

The locked review flow:

Executive Intelligence
↓
Dashboard Understanding
↓
Highest Impact Finding
↓
Priority Actions
↓
Observable Evidence

This becomes a product contract.

7. Success Metrics

Not vanity metrics like downloads.

Real product metrics:

Time to understand review
Time to identify first action
User trust in AI recommendations
Percentage of accepted recommendations
