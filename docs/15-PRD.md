# UX Review Companion — Product Design Document

> The north star for UX Review Companion.
>
> Every feature, prompt, UI decision, and AI engine should align
> with the product direction defined here.

Version: 1.0 (v0.1)

Status: Foundation

---

# 1. Vision

Help product teams identify, explain, prioritize, and improve UX
issues in dashboards using AI.

UX Review Companion is not intended to be:

- Another dashboard analyzer.
- Another screenshot critique tool.

Instead, the product aims to become:

> **An AI UX Consultant.**

The product should help users move from understanding a
dashboard to understanding what matters, why it matters, and
what should be improved next.

---

# 2. Mission

Reduce dashboard review time from:

```text
2–4 hours
     ↓
under 10 minutes
```
