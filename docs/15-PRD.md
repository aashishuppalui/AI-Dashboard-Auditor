📘 UX Review Companion — Product Design Document (PDD)

This will become our "north star." Every feature, prompt, UI, and AI engine should align with it.

1. Vision

Help product teams identify, explain, prioritize, and improve UX issues in dashboards using AI.

Not another dashboard analyzer.

Not another screenshot critique.

Instead, an AI UX Consultant.

2. Mission

Reduce dashboard review time from

2–4 hours → under 10 minutes

while keeping recommendations evidence-based, explainable, and actionable.

9. MVP Success Metrics

Instead of just "it works," let's define measurable goals.

Metric Target
Review generation time < 30 seconds
Dashboard understanding accuracy > 90% (manual evaluation)
Evidence traceability 100% of findings backed by evidence
Priority actions Exactly 3, ranked and actionable
DES consistency Stable across repeated analyses

These give us objective ways to evaluate progress.
