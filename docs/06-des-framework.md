# UX Review Companion — Decision Effectiveness Score (DES)

> Framework for evaluating how effectively a dashboard supports
> its intended decision and workflow.

Version: 1.0 (v0.1)

---

# 1. Purpose

Decision Effectiveness Score (DES) measures how effectively a
dashboard supports the user's intended decision and workflow.

DES is not a visual design score.

DES does not evaluate aesthetics.

DES evaluates decision support.

---

# 2. Definition

DES answers one question:

> **How effectively does this dashboard help its intended user
> make the intended decision?**

The score exists to communicate the effectiveness of the
dashboard as a decision-support experience.

---

# 3. DES Philosophy

The score reflects decision effectiveness, not interface
attractiveness.

A visually attractive dashboard can receive a low DES.

A visually simple dashboard can receive a high DES.

The objective is usefulness.

---

# 4. What DES Evaluates

DES focuses on the quality of decision support provided by
the dashboard.

The current framework evaluates five dimensions:

1. Decision Clarity
2. Information Relevance
3. Information Hierarchy
4. Decision Confidence
5. Actionability

Each dimension contributes up to 20 points.

```text
5 dimensions
×
20 points
=
100 maximum
```
