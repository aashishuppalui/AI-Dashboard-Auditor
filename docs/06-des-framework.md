# Dashboard Effectiveness Score (DES) Framework

Version: 1.0 (MVP)

---

## Purpose

Dashboard Effectiveness Score (DES) measures how effectively a dashboard supports the user's intended decision and workflow.

DES is NOT a visual design score.

DES does NOT evaluate aesthetics.

DES evaluates decision support.

---

## Definition

DES answers one question:

> "How effectively does this dashboard help its intended user make the intended decision?"

---

## DES Philosophy

The score reflects decision effectiveness, not interface attractiveness.

A visually attractive dashboard can receive a low DES.

A visually simple dashboard can receive a high DES.

The objective is usefulness.

---

## Current MVP Formula

Current DES is generated by AI using structured reasoning based on:

- Dashboard understanding
- Observable evidence
- Workflow support
- Information architecture
- Decision support quality

MVP DES is intentionally qualitative.

Future versions will evolve into a weighted scoring model.

---

## Future Scoring Dimensions

The following dimensions are planned but NOT included in MVP.

### Decision Support

Can the dashboard support the intended decision?

### Workflow Support

Does the information follow the user's workflow?

### Information Architecture

Is required information available where needed?

### Context Availability

Is important decision context visible?

### Prioritization

Does the dashboard help users know what matters most?

### Investigation Support

Can users investigate issues without unnecessary context switching?

### Confidence & Uncertainty

Does the dashboard communicate uncertainty where appropriate?

---

## DES Scale

0–40

Poor decision support.

Major redesign required.

41–60

Basic decision support.

Significant workflow improvements recommended.

61–80

Good decision support.

Several opportunities remain.

81–90

Strong dashboard.

Minor improvements.

91–100

Excellent decision-support dashboard.

Best-in-class.

---

## Guiding Principle

DES exists to improve decision-making.

Not visual beauty.
