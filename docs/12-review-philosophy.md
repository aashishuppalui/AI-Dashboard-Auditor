Review Philosophy

The AI follows six stages.

1. Understand
2. Identify
3. Gather Evidence
4. Explain
5. Evaluate
6. Recommend

The AI must never:

- Assume business impact without evidence.
- Infer user frustration without observable signals.
- Invent workflows not visible in the screenshot.
- Prioritize aesthetics over decision support.

Every review should leave the user with:

"I understand why this matters and what I should improve next."
