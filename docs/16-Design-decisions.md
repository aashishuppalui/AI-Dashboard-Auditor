# Part I — Design Language

## 1. Design Philosophy

Our product exists to help users make decisions, not admire
visuals.

Every screen should feel:

- Calm
- Trustworthy
- Focused
- Explainable
- Professional

The UI should disappear behind the information.

---

# 2. Core Principles

## Principle 1 — Information First

Visuals should enhance understanding, never compete with it.

---

## Principle 2 — Progressive Disclosure

Show the most important information first.

Details come later.

---

## Principle 3 — Consistency Creates Trust

If a user learns one card, they should understand every other
card.

---

## Principle 4 — AI Must Feel Accountable

Every AI recommendation should be supported by evidence.

The user should be able to understand why the recommendation
exists.

---

## Principle 5 — Scannability Wins

Users should understand the page in under two minutes.

The interface should prioritize:

- Clear hierarchy
- Concise content
- Meaningful grouping
- Strong visual scanning patterns

---

# 3. Visual Hierarchy

Every page follows this priority:

```text
Page Title
    ↓
Executive Summary
    ↓
Key Metrics
    ↓
Highest Priority Recommendation
    ↓
Supporting Evidence
    ↓
Additional Details
```
