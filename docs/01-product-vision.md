# UX Review Companion — Product Vision

## Vision

Build the world's most trusted AI UX consultant for
decision-support dashboards.

UX Review Companion exists to help teams build dashboards
that improve decision-making — not simply dashboards that
look better.

---

## Mission

**Evidence, not opinions.**

**Improve decisions, not aesthetics.**

UX Review Companion turns dashboard screenshots into
evidence-backed UX reviews that help teams understand
decision effectiveness and determine what to address first.

---

## North Star

> Upload one dashboard.
>
> Receive one trusted AI review backed by observable evidence.

The North Star describes the simplest expression of the
product value.

---

## The Problem We Want to Solve

Dashboard reviews often require significant manual effort.

A useful review needs more than a list of visual problems.
It needs to establish:

- who the dashboard is for
- what decision it supports
- what is visibly present
- what matters most
- what should be addressed first

UX Review Companion aims to make this reasoning faster while
keeping it evidence-backed and explainable.

## Product Direction

The product is intentionally focused on decision-support
dashboards.

It should become trusted not because it produces more
observations, but because its conclusions are:

- relevant
- observable
- explainable
- prioritized
- actionable

---

## Long-Term Vision

The current product begins with dashboard screenshots.

The longer-term direction is broader:

Screenshot Review
↓
Dashboard Review
↓
Application Review
↓
Design System Review
↓
UX Intelligence Platform

The ultimate ambition is to create an AI UX consultant that
can reason about digital experiences through the decisions
they are designed to support.

---

## What We Are Optimizing For

We are not optimizing for:

- the number of findings
- the amount of generated text
- visual design scores
- generic UX recommendations

We are optimizing for:

- decision relevance
- evidence quality
- reasoning traceability
- prioritization
- practical usefulness

---

## Product Principle

The product should help teams answer:

> **What is this experience helping someone decide,
> how well does it support that decision, and what should
> we improve first?**
